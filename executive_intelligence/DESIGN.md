## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, elite visual presence tailored for a business leader operating at the intersection of business administration, data analytics, and artificial intelligence. It merges the institutional polish of top-tier strategy consulting with the precision and clarity of cutting-edge technology platforms. 

The aesthetic is grounded, intellectual, and forward-looking. Recruiter, executive, and venture audiences should perceive rigorous analytical capability, strategic business acumen, and immaculate attention to detail within seconds of landing on the interface.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Tone:** Methodical, sharp, authoritative, and visionary.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Corporate infused with Precision Tech—featuring generous whitespace, disciplined grid discipline, subtle structural borders, and luminous micro-accents.
- **Restraint:** High visual density without clutter; typography and white space carry the visual hierarchy rather than decorative noise.

## Colors

The palette balances clean porcelain foundations with decisive deep-slate ink and a resonant royal-indigo technical accent. The primary cobalt blue conveys algorithmic precision and institutional authority, while secondary deep slates provide high-contrast legibility.

### Surface Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) — Off-white backdrop providing softer ocular contrast than raw white.
- **Card & Surface Raised:** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white surface for distinct card layering and focal prominence.
- **Muted Surface / Code / Stats Well:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) — Subtly tinted container for metrics, tooltips, and data chips.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (Ink):** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) — Maximum contrast for titles, hero headers, and crucial stats.
- **Text Secondary:** `#334155` (Slate 700) — High-legibility body copy and narrative descriptions.
- **Text Muted / Caption:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) — Secondary labels, dates, and auxiliary metadata.

### Accents & Borders
- **Interactive & Focus Primary:** `#1D4ED8` (Blue 700) — Focused, deep corporate royal blue for primary actions and active links.
- **Subtle Highlight / AI Glow:** `#38BDF8` (Sky 400) — Reserved strictly for micro-indicators, sparks, and analytics visualization lines.
- **Hairline Borders:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) — Structural dividers, card borders, and table outlines.
- **Border Hover / Focus:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) — Interactive boundary transitions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs Plus Jakarta Sans for structural headings, metrics, and badges with Inter for long-form readability and case study synthesis.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a contemporary geometric presence with humanist openness, lending an executive yet approachable voice to all titles and KPI counters.
- **Inter** handles body copy, structured analytical findings, and methodology explanations with neutral, high-density legibility.
- **JetBrains Mono** is reserved strictly for algorithmic complexity badges, data parameters, query snippets, and quantitative tech stack tokens.
- **Tight Letter Spacing:** Negative tracking applied to all headings above 20px reinforces an editorial, cohesive finish.

## Layout & Spacing

The portfolio employs a 12-column responsive grid centered inside a maximum container width of `1280px` (`80rem`).

### Grid and Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and generous `space-3xl` (96px) vertical section offsets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `space-2xl` (64px) vertical section pacing.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` outer canvas padding, and `space-xl` (40px) section separation.

### Structure
Content follows strict modular cards. Asymmetrical 8:4 splits are utilized for project highlights: 8 columns for narrative, workflow diagrams, and statistical results; 4 columns for technical specifications, tools utilized (Python, SQL, PyTorch), and recruiter takeaways.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an ambient low-contrast layering method rather than harsh drop shadows. The system pairs delicate 1px perimeter boundaries with diffused, slate-tinted illumination.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` background. Completely flat with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill framed with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and an ambient soft shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Card Hover & Navigation Bar):** Slightly elevated surface for interactive state change: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Executive Overlays, Dropdowns):** Defined focus layer: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 10px 15px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Backdrop Blur Filter:** Floating navigation surfaces use `#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity with an active `12px` Gaussian backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to integrate seamlessly with scrolling content.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, dual-radius approach:

- **Containers & Project Cards:** `1rem` (16px) to `1.25rem` (20px) border radii (`rounded-lg` and custom card token), giving modular content an engineered, approachable presence without excessive playfulness.
- **Pill Badges & Status Indicators:** Full capsule curves (`rounded-full` / 9999px) strictly reserved for category chips, AI capability tags, metric callouts, and secondary action buttons.
- **Input Fields & Button Controls:** `0.5rem` (8px) (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring interactive UI elements remain grounded and distinct from pill-shaped informational metadata.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. On hover, transitions to `#1E40AF` with a soft blue ambient halo (`0 4px 14px 0 rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F1F5F9` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Recruiter CTA ("Download Executive Resume"):** Primary action with an embedded leading icon and subtle rightward arrow animation on hover.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Technical Competency Chip:** Height 28px, full pill shape (`9999px`), `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding. Light blue-slate tint fill (`#EFF6FF`), text `#1D4ED8`, subtle border `1px solid #DBEAFE`.
- **AI / Data Domain Badge:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm`, dot indicator (4px circle) displaying status green `#10B981` (Validated) or blue `#38BDF8` (Model / Production).

### Cards & Project Showcases
- White surface, `16px` or `20px` border-radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. 
- **Internal Padding:** `space-lg` (24px) for compact cards, `space-xl` (40px) for flagship analytics case studies.
- **Card Header Structure:** Category pill badge on top left, publication/completion date right-aligned in `slate-500`. Body features `headline-sm`, two-sentence executive abstract in `body-md`, and a bottom metric counter strip.

### Metrics Counters & Analytical Proof Points
- Vertical stack inside an isolated `#F8FAFC` container.
- **Metric Value:** `stat-counter` token in `#0F172A` with primary accent gradient or pure `#1D4ED8`.
- **Metric Label:** `label-sm` uppercase with wide tracking (`0.05em`), `#64748B`.
- **Context Pill:** Small inline tag indicating delta (e.g., "+34% Efficiency" or "99.2% Model Accuracy").

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, border-radius `8px`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a 3px ring of `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Key Takeaways
- Unordered lists omit standard circular bullets. Instead, utilize custom `16px` SVG chevron or check icons filled with `#1D4ED8` within an enclosed `24px` circular slate background.
